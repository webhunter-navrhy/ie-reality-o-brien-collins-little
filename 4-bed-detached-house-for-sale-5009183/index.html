Description

Attractive 4 bedroom detached home in Grangerath.

No. 3 Park Rise is a beautifully presented four-bedroom detached family home offering spacious, well-balanced accommodation in a superb setting. This impressive property has been thoughtfully designed to cater for modern family living, with bright, generously proportioned rooms throughout.

The ground floor comprises a stunning double height entrance hall with a feature window leading to two elegant reception rooms, providing flexible living space. At the heart of the home is a spacious open-plan kitchen and dining room, ideal for everyday family life. A separate utility room and guest WC complete the ground floor accommodation.

Upstairs, there are four generously sized bedrooms, including a beautiful main bedroom with floor to ceiling mirrored wardrobes and an en-suite shower room. A stylish family bathroom serves the remaining bedrooms, ensuring ample space and comfort for growing families.

Externally, the property enjoys excellent kerb appeal with a cobblelock driveway providing off-street parking for two cars. To the rear, the private south-west facing garden boasts a decked patio area that is perfect for outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply enjoying the afternoon and evening sunshine.

Ideally located close to a host of local amenities, schools, shops and transport links, this exceptional home offers everything a discerning purchaser could wish for.

Viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate all that this wonderful family home has to offer.
